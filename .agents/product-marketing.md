## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Fixed-price websites, SEO, website support and AI agents for businesses in Iraq, Oman and the Gulf, done fully online and paid in Tether (USDT).
**What it does:** Designs bilingual (Arabic/English, also Persian/Dari) business websites, catalogs and stores; runs SEO; maintains sites; builds and supports WhatsApp/Telegram/web AI agents; designs mobile apps.
**Product category:** Web design & digital marketing agency (MEFTACARD Studio).
**Product type:** Productised service with three fixed packages plus monthly care plans.
**Business model:** One-time project fee + optional monthly care plan. Packages: Local $1,250 (launch 14 days, 30 days support), Trade & Export $2,250 (30 days, 60 days support, team training, in-person support), Enterprise from $25,000 (12–16 weeks, by milestone, 24/7 support). Payment in USDT on TRC-20/ERC-20; Local & Trade half at start, half at launch; Enterprise per approved milestone. Monthly care-plan price: quoted per site (no public price yet).

**Decision-makers:** Owners and general managers; marketing managers at larger firms; procurement/IT at Enterprise (oil & gas, industrial, groups).

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Customers search on phones in Arabic and message on WhatsApp; most regional business sites are English-only, slow on mobile, without WhatsApp/call buttons or local SEO, so enquiries go to competitors.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Freelancers: low price but changing scope, machine-translated Arabic, unclear ownership, little support.
- Large agencies: expensive, often hourly, slow.
- Website builders/templates: generic, flipped RTL layouts, weak SEO.
**What it costs them:** Lost enquiries every day; low trust with foreign partners.
**Emotional tension:** Fear of paying and getting nothing (or losing access to their own site); difficulty paying foreign vendors.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Local/regional web agencies in Baghdad, Erbil, Muscat, Dubai — often hourly or vague scope, bank transfer only.
**Secondary:** Freelancers (Upwork/Instagram) — cheap, inconsistent, weak Arabic.
**Indirect:** Instagram-only presence; DIY builders (Wix/WordPress themes).

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Fixed price, written scope and launch date before work starts.
- Pay in USDT from any country — no international bank transfer or foreign card.
- Native Arabic (and Persian/Dari) pages, real RTL design.

- Fully online; in-person support on Trade/Enterprise.
**How we do it differently:** Productised packages, WhatsApp-first conversion design, SEO built in from day one.
**Why that's better:** Predictable cost and timing; more enquiries per visitor.

## Brand Voice
| Attribute | Means | Doesn't mean | Example line |
|-----------|-------|--------------|--------------|
| Direct | Plain, specific, short | Blunt or salesy | "Your homepage has no WhatsApp button." |
| Honest | Verifiable claims only | Hedging everything | "No one can guarantee page one; we guarantee the work." |
| Warm | Respectful, regional courtesy | Over-familiar | "السلام عليكم فريق …" |

**Tone by context:**
- Support: calm, step-by-step.
- Launch: confident, factual.
- Errors: own it, fix it.
- Sales: value first (free review), one clear next step, no fake urgency.
- Social: useful tips, no hype.
**Terms we own:** "Free website review", "fixed price, written scope".
**Never use (and why):** fake testimonials/client logos (trust and legality); "Concept" work presented as client work.
