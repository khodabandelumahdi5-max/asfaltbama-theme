Asphalt for Iraqi summers

In Baghdad and Basra, pavement temperatures in summer go far above air temperature. Mixes made with soft bitumen, too much binder or weak bases develop ruts and shoving where trucks brake and turn. For industrial yards we recommend:

  • A properly compacted granular base on stable subgrade
  • Two asphalt layers (binder and wearing course) where trucks and trailers run
  • A bitumen grade suited to hot climates, as the project specification allows
  • Falls and drains so that rainwater and wash water leave the surface quickly

Roof waterproofing

Flat concrete roofs of factories, warehouses and offices need a continuous membrane, correct falls to the outlets and careful detailing around equipment bases, ducts and parapets. Exposed membranes should have a UV-resistant or reflective finish; bitumen and jute systems need a protection layer. Every roof is water-tested before handover.
