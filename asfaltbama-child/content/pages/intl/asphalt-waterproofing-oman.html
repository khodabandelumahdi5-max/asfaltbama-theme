Asfaltbama takes on asphalt paving and roof waterproofing projects in the Sultanate of Oman: industrial yards and warehouses in the industrial estates, access roads, car parks, villas and the flat roofs of commercial and industrial buildings. Oman combines intense heat and coastal humidity with short, heavy rainstorms, so drainage matters as much as the pavement itself.

Drainage first

When heavy rain comes, water that cannot leave a pavement soaks into the base and weakens it, and ponding on flat roofs finds every weak joint. On every project we check levels and outlets before paving or roofing: falls built into the base, gullies and channels set slightly below the finished surface, and roof falls leading to working outlets.

Heat and humidity

  • Pavements: strong base layers, full compaction and bitumen grades suited to high temperatures, as the specification allows.
  • Roofs: UV-resistant or reflective membranes, or a protection layer, to slow ageing under the sun.
  • Coastal sites: surfaces must be clean and dry before priming and membrane application; humidity is managed by timing the works.
