Asfaltbama (Ofogh Apadana Pasargad Co.) is an asphalt paving and waterproofing contractor with 25 years of experience in Tehran and Alborz, Iran. We take on industrial and commercial projects in Iraq, Oman and the United Arab Emirates: asphalt paving of factory yards, warehouses and access roads, bitumen roofing and APP modified bitumen membrane (isogam) waterproofing, and supply of bitumen and waterproofing materials.

What we do

  • Asphalt paving: subgrade preparation, base course, binder and wearing courses laid with paver and rollers
  • Industrial yards: heavy-duty pavements for trucks, trailers and forklifts, loading areas and car parks
  • Roof waterproofing: torch-applied modified bitumen membranes and two-layer hot bitumen and jute systems
  • Repair and maintenance: patching, milling and overlay, hot-poured crack sealing
  • Materials supply: bitumen and waterproofing membranes for contractors

How we work on projects abroad

  1. Enquiry: send drawings, photos, approximate areas and the location by email or WhatsApp.
  2. Technical review: we review the scope, the specification and the local conditions (climate, traffic loads, drainage).
  3. Proposal: method statement, layer thicknesses, materials, schedule and price, in writing.
  4. Mobilisation: our site team, and materials or equipment as agreed, working under the client's or main contractor's specification and approvals.
  5. Execution and handover: quality checks during the works, water testing of roofs and a written warranty.

Designed for hot climates

Summer surface temperatures in the region are among the highest in the world. Soft bitumen and poorly compacted mixes rut and shove under trucks, and exposed roofing ages quickly under the sun. We propose harder or polymer-modified bitumen grades where the specification allows, strong base layers, correct compaction while the mix is hot, and reflective, UV-resistant membranes or protection layers on roofs.
