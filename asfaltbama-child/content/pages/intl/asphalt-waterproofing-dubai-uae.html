Asfaltbama takes on asphalt paving and roof waterproofing work in Dubai and the United Arab Emirates: warehouse and factory yards, logistics and industrial areas, car parks, access roads, villa driveways and the flat roofs of commercial and industrial buildings. We work to the project specification and within the approvals of the main contractor, consultant and local authorities.

Our services in the UAE

  • Asphalt paving of warehouse and factory yards, car parks and access roads
  • Heavy-duty pavements for trucks, trailers and forklifts
  • Roof waterproofing with modified bitumen membranes and bitumen-based systems
  • Milling and overlay, patching and crack sealing; supply of waterproofing materials

Built for the UAE climate

  • Rutting resistance: truck routes and loading bays need a strong base, a binder course and a bitumen grade suited to very high pavement temperatures, as specified.
  • Compaction: rolling is completed while the mix is at the right temperature; in summer, work is scheduled for the cooler hours.
  • Roofs under sun: reflective or UV-resistant membranes, or a protection layer, slow ageing and reduce heat load.
  • Drainage: falls and outlets are checked before paving and roofing so that rare but heavy rain leaves quickly.

Areas

Dubai (including industrial and logistics areas), Sharjah, Ajman and the northern emirates, depending on project size.

Working with main contractors

For projects in the UAE we usually work as a specialist subcontractor: we submit method statements and material data for approval, follow the consultant's inspection and test plan and hand over with water-test records for roofs.

Frequently asked questions

Can you work at night?

Yes. In summer, paving is often scheduled for night or early morning to control mix temperature and site conditions, subject to site rules.

Do you supply materials?

Yes, bitumen and waterproofing membranes can be supplied; quantities and delivery are agreed per order.
