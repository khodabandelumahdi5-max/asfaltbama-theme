Do you build in Arabic and English?

Yes. Every package ships in both, with right-to-left Arabic design, separate pages for each language and the search setup Google needs to show the right one.

How do we pay?

In USDT (Tether), on TRC-20 or ERC-20. Local and Trade are paid half at the start and half at launch; Enterprise is paid per approved milestone.

Do you offer in-person support?

Yes, on the Trade & Export and Enterprise packages: an on-site kickoff, team training and support visits. The Local package is supported remotely.

Which countries do you work in?

Iraq, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain and Lebanon. Because we work online, the country doesn't limit what we can do.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. Everything, from the first call to design reviews, launch and support, is done online. In-person support is available on the Trade & Export and Enterprise packages if you want it.

Can we start small and grow later?

Yes. Local and Trade sites are built on the same foundation as Enterprise, so nothing is thrown away when you upgrade.

Who owns the site when it's finished?

You do, from day one: code, domain, hosting, analytics and ad accounts.
